One Screen, Complete School Control

A 10-minute demonstration on your own students' data will communicate more than any written proposal. Below is what the system does, and — more importantly — what it saves.

Feature to Benefit

Har feature ka business impact

QR Student ID Card & Scan-Based Attendance
Teacher attendance marking in under a second — no register, no pen, no manual copying. Har student ko apna printed QR ID card milta hai, jise class mein scan karwana hota hai.
Auto Student ID & Permanent Digital Record
Admission par Student ID automatically generate hota hai (STU-1001, STU-1002...). Name, father, phone, class, fee history — sab ek record mein. Saal baad bhi 1 click mein milta hai.
Fee Tracking & Outstanding Balance
Kisi bhi waqt dekhein kis class mein kitni fee baaki hai. Partial payments, remaining, monthly status — sab hisaab live rehta hai. Koe outstanding balance "yaad se" nahi chhutega.
Proper Payment Receipts
Har payment par professional receipt print ho sakta hai. "Already paid" ke disputes khatam — school ke hisaab ki accountability document-supported ho jati hai.
Daily & Monthly Attendance Reports
Daily sheet, monthly percentage, class-wise summary — sab printable/PDF. Board visit, parent meeting ya monthly summary ke liye ready record pehle se tayar.
Student Portal — visibility for students & parents
Student apne login se attendance, fee status aur apna QR card dekh sakta hai. Parents ko daily visibility milti hai — ye trust directly branding ko support karta hai.
Data Backup & Security
Records alag location par backup hote hain. Hardware failure ya virus ka risk khatam — data continuity is school ki asli security hai.
